Four things hold for every change below:

- **No Tab stop is added.** The report proposes `tabindex="0"` for F-001, F-003, F-007 and F-009.
  That adds a Tab stop, and with no key handler Enter and Space would do nothing on it. These
  changes give the role and the name only; keyboard access is a choice for you ("Needs your
  decision", first item).
- **No label starts acting on a field.** Correcting a `for` that names nothing, or names the wrong
  field, makes a click on the label focus (or toggle) the field. Where a label names nothing or
  the wrong thing, the change names the field with `aria-labelledby` and leaves what a click on the
  label does as it is. Correcting the `for`s is a choice for you ("Needs your decision").
- **`data-agentom-*` attributes with meaning** (`-risk`, `-confirmation`, `-action`, `-requires`,
  `-reveals`, `-kind`, `-value`) always sit beside a `data-agentom-id`. `data-agentom-redact` and
  `data-agentom-ignore` work on any element, and on an element around a field (the projector
  looks up with `closest()`), so on a wrapper component's root they cover what's inside it.
- **Names go through `$t`**, with the new keys in C-1. vue-i18n falls back to English
  (`fallbackLocale: "en"`, src/i18n.js:106), so the other languages read the English text until
  they are translated.
